# 📊 Sales & Profit Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes sales and profit performance across products, regions, and time.

## 🎯 Objective
To help a business understand what is driving sales and profit, and where to improve.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop
- DAX (measures & calculated columns)
- Power Query (data cleaning)

## 📈 Key Insights
- West region generated the highest profit, followed by East, South and Central.
- Sales showed steady growth from 2016 to 2019.
- Total sales reached 2.30M with a total profit of 286K.
